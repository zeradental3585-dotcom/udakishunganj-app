Udakishunganj Subdivision Guide: The Blocks Under Its Administration

Updated July 2026 · Directory

This page compiles publicly available administrative and government-portal information as of July 2026. Panchayat boundaries, office contacts, and scheme rules can change — always confirm current details with the Udakishunganj Block/Anchal office or the official portal linked below before relying on them.

Udakishunganj is not only a community development block in its own right, it is also the headquarters town of Udakishunganj subdivision — one of the administrative subdivisions of Madhepura district, Bihar, sitting in the alluvial plains of the Kosi river basin in northeastern Bihar. A Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) based in Udakishunganj oversees subdivision-level administration.

Official source: madhepura.nic.in's "Subdivision & Blocks" page lists the current, authoritative breakdown of Madhepura district's subdivisions and the blocks under each — cross-check there if you need the current administrative map for any official purpose.

Blocks under Udakishunganj subdivision

According to publicly available administrative records, Udakishunganj subdivision's community development blocks include Gwalpara, Bihariganj, Alamnagar, Kishanganj, Chausa, and Puraini, in addition to Udakishunganj block itself where the subdivision headquarters sits. Because block boundaries and subdivision groupings are occasionally reorganised by the state, residents should treat this as a general reference and confirm current groupings with madhepura.nic.in for anything official.

Why the subdivision matters day-to-day

  • SDO-level matters: certain certificates, appeals, and administrative matters escalate from the block/Circle Office to the Sub-Divisional Officer in Udakishunganj rather than going straight to the district headquarters in Madhepura town.
  • Courts and registration: some subdivision-level government offices (registration, sub-divisional courts where applicable) are based in or near Udakishunganj town, serving the whole subdivision.
  • Local identity: being a subdivision headquarters gives Udakishunganj town a larger administrative and commercial footprint than a typical block headquarters in the district.
Tip: if you're not sure whether a matter should go to your panchayat, the Udakishunganj block office, or the subdivision (SDO) office, the block office is almost always the correct first stop — they will direct you upward if needed.

The bottom line

Udakishunganj serves double duty as both a block and a subdivision headquarters within Madhepura district — useful context for understanding why some government offices and services are based in the town beyond what a typical block would have.
