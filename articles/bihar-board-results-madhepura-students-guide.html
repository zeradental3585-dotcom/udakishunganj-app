Bihar Board Results & School Admission Updates for Madhepura District Students

Updated July 2026 · Education

This page compiles publicly available administrative and government-portal information as of July 2026. Panchayat boundaries, office contacts, and scheme rules can change — always confirm current details with the Udakishunganj Block/Anchal office or the official portal linked below before relying on them.

Students in Udakishunganj block and the wider Madhepura district follow the same statewide process as every other Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) student to check 10th and 12th results, download admit cards, and apply for further education — the only difference is which local schools and colleges are the practical next step.

Official result websites: results.biharboardonline.com and biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in for BSEB 10th/12th results and admit cards.

Checking your result

  1. Visit the official BSEB result website.
  2. Select the 10th or 12th result link for the relevant year.
  3. Enter your roll code and roll number exactly as printed on your admit card.
  4. Download and save your provisional marksheet.

Local next steps after 12th

Madhepura district is home to Bhupendra Narayan Mandal University, the region's state university, with affiliated colleges serving students across the district including those from Udakishunganj block. Most undergraduate admissions to government and many private colleges run through Bihar's centralized OFSS (Online Facilitation System for Students) portal.

For deeper exam coverage: our sister site KosiEducationTimes.com publishes detailed, regularly updated guides on BSEB results, admit cards, scholarships, and Bihar government job (sarkari naukri) recruitment for the wider Kosi-region districts — useful if you want exam-cycle detail beyond what's covered here.

Scholarships for Madhepura district students

Students who qualify by category (SC/ST/BC/EBC) and income can apply for the Bihar Post Matric Scholarship through scstpmsonline.bihar.gov.in (SC/ST) or pmsonline.bihar.gov.in (BC/EBC) once enrolled beyond class 10 — the application window typically runs from mid-July to mid-September each year.

The bottom line

Udakishunganj-area students use the same official BSEB and OFSS systems as the rest of Bihar; Bhupendra Narayan Mandal University in Madhepura town is the nearest state university anchor for undergraduate study.
