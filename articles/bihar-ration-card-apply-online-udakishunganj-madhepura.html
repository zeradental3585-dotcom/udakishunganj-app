Bihar Ration Card Online Apply 2026: Guide for Udakishunganj & Madhepura Residents

Updated July 2026 · Civic

This page compiles publicly available administrative and government-portal information as of July 2026. Panchayat boundaries, office contacts, and scheme rules can change — always confirm current details with the Udakishunganj Block/Anchal office or the official portal linked below before relying on them.

Bihar's ration card system runs on the state's Electronic Public Distribution System (EPDS), through which residents of Udakishunganj block and the rest of Madhepura district can apply for a new ration card, check application status, download an e-card, and complete mandatory e-KYC for existing cards.

Official portals: the Bihar government's own portal is rconline.bihar.gov.in / epds.bihar.gov.in for ration card services. Several private/aggregator sites rank highly in search results and imitate the official look — always check the URL ends in a bihar.gov.in government domain before entering personal details or Aadhaar information.

Applying for a new ration card

  1. Visit the official EPDS Bihar / rconline portal.
  2. Register using your mobile number and complete OTP verification.
  3. Fill the household details form — head of family, all members' Aadhaar numbers, income category (AAY/PHH/other), and current address (Udakishunganj block, Madhepura district, PIN 852220).
  4. Upload required documents: Aadhaar cards of all members, income certificate, residence proof, and a passport-size photo of the head of household.
  5. Submit — the application is then verified at the panchayat/block level before approval.

e-KYC for existing ration cards

Bihar has been running e-KYC verification drives requiring existing ration card holders to link Aadhaar and complete biometric or OTP-based e-KYC to keep their card active. Cards without completed e-KYC risk being flagged or temporarily suspended in distribution records — check your card's status on the portal and complete this promptly if you haven't already.

Udakishunganj tip: if the online process is confusing or your mobile number isn't linked to Aadhaar, your local Public Distribution System (PDS) dealer or the panchayat sevak can usually guide you through the process in person — this is often faster than troubleshooting portal errors remotely.

Checking application or card status

Use the "RC Details" or "Application Status" option on the EPDS Bihar portal with your application/reference number or Aadhaar number to check progress. Processing can take a few weeks depending on panchayat-level verification workload.

The bottom line

Always use the official bihar.gov.in ration card domains, keep Aadhaar linked and e-KYC current, and use your local PDS dealer or panchayat office as a first stop if you get stuck — this avoids both scam sites and unnecessary delays.
