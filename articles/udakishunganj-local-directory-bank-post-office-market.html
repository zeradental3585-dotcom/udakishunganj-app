Udakishunganj Local Directory: Bank, Post Office, Market & Healthcare

Updated July 2026 · Directory

This page compiles publicly available administrative and government-portal information as of July 2026. Panchayat boundaries, office contacts, and scheme rules can change — always confirm current details with the Udakishunganj Block/Anchal office or the official portal linked below before relying on them.

As a subdivision headquarters town, Udakishunganj carries more everyday infrastructure than a typical village in the block — a local market, banking access, a post office, and basic healthcare. Here's what residents and visitors typically look for.

Banking

Udakishunganj town has bank branches and business correspondent (BC) points that serve the surrounding panchayats for basic banking, Direct Benefit Transfer withdrawals, and passbook services. For a specific bank branch, IFSC code, or working hours, check the bank's own branch locator (most major nationalised banks operating in Bihar have one) rather than relying on outdated listings, since branch details change.

Post office

The Udakishunganj post office (PIN 852220) handles standard postal services, Post Office Savings Bank accounts, and government scheme-linked postal products like Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana, common across rural Bihar post offices.

Local market

Udakishunganj's town market serves as the commercial hub for the block, with a weekly haat (market day) pattern typical of small Bihar towns, alongside daily general stores, agricultural input shops, and small eateries.

Healthcare: for anything beyond basic care, the nearest higher-level facility is typically the Sadar Hospital in Madhepura town (the district headquarters) or Bhupendra Narayan Mandal Medical College Hospital, also in Madhepura — both roughly 35 km from Udakishunganj. Local Primary Health Centres (PHC) and Anganwadi-linked health referrals handle routine and preventive care closer to home.

Getting around

Udakishunganj is connected to Madhepura town and other Kosi-region towns by state highway and district road links; local transport is largely by shared auto/tempo and bus, typical of the wider Kosi river basin region.

The bottom line

Udakishunganj town covers most day-to-day needs locally; for specialised healthcare, banking disputes, or higher education, Madhepura town (the district headquarters) is the nearest larger hub, about 35 km away.
