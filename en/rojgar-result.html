Government jobs & results: right portals, age calculator & document list

Use only official websites for recruitment, results and admit cards. Here are the right portals, an age-check tool and a preparation checklist for youth of Udakishunganj and the Kosi region.

How to avoid fake websites

Keep in mind: a genuine recruitment address ends in .gov.in or .nic.in (or .ac.in for universities). No real recruitment asks for money, OTPs or Aadhaar/bank details on WhatsApp or Telegram. Claims like "100% selection" or "guaranteed seat" are signs of fraud. Pay form fees only on the official portal.

Official portals

Bihar government jobs

  • BPSC — Bihar Public Service Commission
  • BSSC — Bihar Staff Selection Commission
  • Bihar Police recruitment (CSBC)
  • BTSC — Bihar Technical Service Commission

Central govt, banking & railways

  • UPSC — Union Public Service Commission
  • SSC — Staff Selection Commission
  • IBPS — bank recruitment
  • Indian Railways (RRB links from here)
  • National Career Service (govt job portal)

Results & admit cards

  • Bihar Board (BSEB) results
  • Bihar Board online portal
  • BNMU (Madhepura) — notices
  • Post-matric scholarship
