Bihar 125 Units Free Electricity: How You Get a Zero Bill and Who Misses Out (Udakishunganj & Madhepura, NBPDCL)

Updated November 2026 · Civic

This page is based on publicly available news and scheme-portal information as of November 2026. Sources differ on the start date, tenant rules and extra charges — confirm your bill and the final rules with the NBPDCL consumer centre or website.

The Bihar government announced up to 125 free units of electricity every month for domestic consumers, and reports say it has been built into the power companies' billing systems. Madhepura district falls under the North Bihar Power Distribution Company (NBPDCL), so bills for Udakishunganj consumers come from NBPDCL's system.

Official source: NBPDCL — nbpdcl.co.in — for viewing bills, payment and consumer services. Check any official notification on this scheme there or at your power consumer centre.

Do you need to apply?

Reports say no separate application or registration is needed. If your monthly consumption stays within the limit (125 units), the bill shows a zero payable amount. The benefit is applied automatically through billing.

What if you use more than 125 units?

According to some reports, energy charges and electricity duty apply on units above the limit, and a fixed charge may apply. For an exact figure, read your bill's breakdown or ask the consumer centre.

Who may face problems

  • Tenants / sub-meters: some reports say people on sub-meters do not get the benefit, while others say tenants with a valid separate connection may. If the rule is unclear, ask your consumer centre.
  • Commercial connections: the scheme is for domestic consumers, not shops or commercial connections.
  • New connections: reports mention verification of new-connection applications — make sure the connection is in your name.

If your bill looks wrong

  1. Check the consumer number, units consumed and payable amount on the bill
  2. Visit the nearest NBPDCL consumer centre/office with the bill and connection papers and file a written complaint
  3. Get your mobile number updated on your electricity account so you receive SMS alerts
  4. If it isn't resolved, escalate to a senior officer or the consumer grievance forum
Keep in mind: beware of links or calls that ask for money or OTPs for "free electricity registration". The genuine process normally needs no separate application.
