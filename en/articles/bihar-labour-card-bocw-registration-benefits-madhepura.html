Bihar Labour Card (BOCW): Registration, Eligibility & Benefits — for Construction Workers in Udakishunganj & Madhepura

Updated November 2026 · Scheme

This page is based on the Bihar BOCW Welfare Board portal and other public sources as of November 2026. Sources differ on age limits, work-experience rules, fees and benefit amounts — check bocw.bihar.gov.in for current rules before applying.

The Bihar Labour Card is proof of registration for construction workers with the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers (BOCW) Welfare Board. Reports say it can open access to welfare schemes such as education help, pension, accident/health support, assistance to buy tools and a bicycle grant. In Madhepura district, masons, labourers, carpenters, painters, electricians and other construction workers may fall under it.

Official source: bocw.bihar.gov.in — the Bihar BOCW Welfare Board portal, with options for labour registration and checking application status.

Who may be eligible

  • A permanent resident of Bihar
  • A worker engaged in building or construction work
  • Age is generally stated as 18 to 60 years
  • Some sources mention a 90-day work-experience rule — this could not be confirmed officially, so check with the Board

Documents you need

  • Aadhaar card
  • Passport-size photo
  • Proof of residence and age
  • Bank account details
  • Work-experience proof (certified by employer/contractor/relevant official, where required)

How to apply

On the portal, go to "Labour Registration", choose new registration, verify Aadhaar, fill in the details and upload the documents. Application status can be checked on the portal's "Application Status" page. You can also take help from a nearby CSC. Registration/renewal fees follow the Board's rules.

Labour card vs e-Shram card

The labour card is the state BOCW Board's registration for construction workers, whereas the e-Shram card is the central Labour Ministry's portal for unorganised workers. They are separate; construction workers benefit from learning about both.

Keep in mind: be careful if brokers or agents ask for money to make a labour card. Apply only on the official portal or at a CSC, and never share OTPs or bank details.

The bottom line

Construction workers should apply through the official portal and confirm the eligibility conditions with the Board. For other schemes, try our scheme checker.
