Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY): Guide for Madhepura Farmers — Protection from Kosi Floods

Updated September 2026 · Scheme

This information is based on publicly available details on the PMFBY portal of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare as of September 2026. Premium rates, crops, and cut-off dates vary by district and season — always check pmfby.gov.in for current information before applying.

Madhepura district lies in the Kosi river's flood-affected area, where farmers face the risk of crop damage from floods or unusual rainfall every year. The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) gives farmers crop insurance cover at a very low premium — compensation is paid for losses from floods, drought, pest attacks, or other natural calamities.

Official source: pmfby.gov.in — the official portal of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India. District-wise premium rates and cut-off dates are listed here.

Premium rates (farmer's share)

Crop categoryFarmer's premium
Kharif crops (paddy, etc.)2% of sum insured
Rabi crops (wheat, pulses, oilseeds)1.5% of sum insured
Commercial/horticultural crops5% of sum insured

The remaining premium is shared between the central and state governments. For example, if the sum insured for paddy is ₹50,000 per hectare, the farmer pays only about ₹1,000 per hectare in premium.

How to apply

  • Register yourself on pmfby.gov.in, or apply through your nearest bank branch or Common Service Centre (CSC)
  • Submit Aadhaar, bank account details, land documents (khata-khesra/jamabandi), and a sowing certificate
  • Pay the fixed premium amount based on your crop and season (Kharif/Rabi)
  • Compensation is calculated automatically based on Crop Cutting Experiments or a notified calamity

How claims are paid

If widespread damage occurs due to a notified natural calamity (such as a Kosi flood), the state government and insurance company jointly calculate compensation for the affected area based on Crop Cutting Experiments, and the amount is transferred directly to the farmer's bank account via DBT. For localised damage (such as hailstorm damage to an individual field), you must report it within 72 hours via the toll-free number or portal.

Required documents

  • Aadhaar card
  • Bank passbook
  • Khata-khesra/jamabandi number (land records)
  • Sowing certificate (issued by the government patwari/agriculture coordinator)
Keep in mind: crop insurance applications have a fixed cut-off date that differs between the Kharif and Rabi seasons. Missing the deadline means no cover for that season — always check the current date for Madhepura district on pmfby.gov.in.

The bottom line

For farmers in the Kosi belt, PMFBY is an essential safeguard against losses from floods and other natural disasters. For land records, see our Bihar land records guide, and for flood safety information, see our Kosi flood alert guide.
