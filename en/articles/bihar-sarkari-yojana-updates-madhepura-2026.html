Bihar Sarkari Yojana Updates 2026: What Udakishunganj & Madhepura Residents Should Know

Updated July 2026 · Scheme

This page compiles publicly available administrative and government-portal information as of July 2026. Panchayat boundaries, office contacts, and scheme rules can change — always confirm current details with the Udakishunganj Block/Anchal office or the official portal linked below before relying on them.

Several central and Bihar state government welfare schemes are actively rolled out at the panchayat level in Udakishunganj block, as elsewhere in rural Madhepura district. Here's a round-up of the major ones and where to start.

SchemeWhat it coversWhere to start
PM Awas Yojana (Gramin)Housing assistance for eligible rural householdsPanchayat/block office; PMAY-G portal
Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)Free hospital treatment up to ₹5 lakh/year per familypmjay.gov.in / nearest Common Service Centre (CSC)
Ujjwala YojanaSubsidised LPG gas connections for BPL householdsNearest LPG distributor with required documents
Har Ghar Nal Ka JalPiped drinking water connection to rural householdsPanchayat/PHED (Public Health Engineering Department)
MGNREGA100 days guaranteed rural wage employment per householdPanchayat office (job card registration)
Common Service Centres (CSC): many of these schemes can be applied for or checked through a local CSC (Jan Seva Kendra) operator, who can handle the online application and document upload for a small service fee where the government doesn't provide it free.

Documents typically needed across schemes

  • Aadhaar card (all household members)
  • Ration card / BPL or income certificate
  • Bank account linked to Aadhaar (for Direct Benefit Transfer)
  • Residence proof showing your panchayat and block (Udakishunganj)
Udakishunganj tip: your gram panchayat's mukhiya or panchayat sevak keeps track of which households have already been enrolled in major schemes like PMAY-G and Ayushman Bharat — check with them before assuming you need to start a fresh application, since duplicate applications can cause delays.

Avoiding scheme-related scams

None of these schemes require an upfront "processing fee" paid to an individual — CSC service charges are small and regulated. Be cautious of anyone asking for a large payment to "guarantee" scheme approval; report such requests to the block office.

The bottom line

Most major welfare schemes touching Udakishunganj households run through the panchayat and block office or a local CSC — starting there is faster and safer than navigating unfamiliar portals alone.
