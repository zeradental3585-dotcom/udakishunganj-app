About Udakishunganj: History, Geography & the Kosi River

Updated July 2026 · Background

Udakishunganj is a community development block and subdivision headquarters town in Madhepura district, Bihar, situated in the alluvial plains of the Kosi river basin in northeastern Bihar. The Kosi river — often called the "Sorrow of Bihar" for its history of dramatic course changes and flooding — has shaped much of the region's settlement patterns, agriculture, and infrastructure planning for generations.

Administrative position

Udakishunganj serves a dual administrative role: it is both a community development block in its own right and the headquarters of Udakishunganj subdivision, which groups together several neighbouring blocks under a Sub-Divisional Officer based in the town. The block itself is organised into gram panchayats covering roughly 44 villages — see our full panchayat and village list.

Population

Published census figures put Udakishunganj block's population at approximately 136,937 as of the 2001 census; more recent block-level figures from the 2011 census (or later) were not available from the sources consulted for this page. For current, authoritative population data, the District Census Handbook for Madhepura, published by the Census of India / Directorate of Census Operations, Bihar, is the definitive source.
