Mukhyamantri Mahila Rojgar Yojana, Bihar: How to Get ₹10,000, Eligibility & Apply (Udakishunganj & Madhepura)

Updated November 2026 · Scheme

This page is based on publicly available news and scheme-portal information as of November 2026. Sources differ on amounts, stages and application links — confirm current rules with JEEViKA (BRLPS) or your block JEEViKA office before applying.

The Mukhyamantri Mahila Rojgar Yojana is a Bihar government scheme that gives eligible women a ₹10,000 starting grant, paid directly to their bank account, to begin or grow a small livelihood. It is described as a starting grant, not a loan. Rural women in Udakishunganj and Madhepura mostly join through JEEViKA self-help groups.

Official source: Bihar Rural Livelihoods Promotion Society (JEEViKA / BRLPS) — brlps.in. Sources list different application links, so get the correct link directly from brlps.in or from your Jeevika Didi / Community Mobiliser (CM).

Who may be eligible

  • A woman who is a permanent resident of Bihar
  • Roughly 18 to 60 years of age (sources give this range)
  • Usually one woman per family gets the benefit once
  • A plan to start or expand a small livelihood (tailoring, grocery, livestock, vegetables, handicrafts, etc.)

Documents you need

  • Aadhaar card
  • Address / residence proof
  • Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked, active account)
  • Passport-size photo
  • A short description of the planned livelihood or business

How to apply

In rural areas most women apply through their JEEViKA self-help group (SHG) and village organisation (VO) — the Jeevika Didi or Community Mobiliser forwards your form. An online process is described for urban women. To learn the right route for your area, contact the JEEViKA Block Project Manager (BPM) at the Block office in Udakishunganj.

Support after the first grant

Several reports say further assistance may follow after the business is reviewed, but sources disagree on the amount, timing and conditions. Before relying on any larger figure, get official confirmation from the JEEViKA office.

Keep in mind: if any agent asks for a form fee or "setting" money in the name of this scheme, be careful — government scheme applications do not need bribes or extra fees. Never share OTPs, bank passwords or Aadhaar photos with unknown people or WhatsApp numbers.

The bottom line

Keep your bank account linked to Aadhaar, join a JEEViKA group and keep your documents ready. To see which other schemes you may qualify for, try our scheme checker, or read the e-Shram card guide.
