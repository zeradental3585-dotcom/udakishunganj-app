MGNREGA Job Card in Udakishunganj & Madhepura: How to Apply & Current Wage Rate

Updated August 2026 · Scheme

This page compiles publicly available information from the Ministry of Rural Development and the MGNREGA portal as of August 2026. Wage rates and rules are updated by the state government each financial year — check nrega.nic.in for the latest figures.

Under the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA), any adult in a rural household can claim up to 100 days of guaranteed unskilled manual work per financial year. The scheme applies across all panchayats in Udakishunganj block, and getting a job card is completely free.

Official source: nrega.nic.in (the central MGNREGA portal) publishes job card holder lists, work details, and wage payment information for Bihar. Applications are made via the UMANG app or the local Gram Panchayat office.

How to apply for a job card

  • Online (UMANG app): download the app, register with your mobile number and OTP, search "MGNREGA" and select "Apply for Job Card", then fill in state-district-block-panchayat
  • Offline: submit an application at your Gram Panchayat office with identity proof, address proof, and a photo — the Gram Rozgar Sevak processes the form
  • Any rural resident aged 18 or above can apply, with no income limit or caste restriction
  • Job cards are typically issued within 7–15 days after verification

Wage rate and payment

For financial year 2025–26, Bihar's MGNREGA daily wage rate is ₹245 (revised annually by the state government). Wages are credited directly to the worker's Aadhaar-linked bank account. Work is made available at the panchayat level — such as pond digging, road repair, and plantation drives.

Keep in mind: if work isn't provided within 15 days of a request, workers are entitled to an unemployment allowance. For delayed or missing wage payments, file a complaint at your Gram Panchayat or Block office.

The bottom line

The MGNREGA job card is an important safety net for rural households. For more panchayat-level information, see our panchayat & village list guide.
