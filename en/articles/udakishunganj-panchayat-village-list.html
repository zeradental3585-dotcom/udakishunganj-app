Udakishunganj Block: Panchayat & Village List

Updated July 2026 · Directory

This page compiles publicly available administrative and government-portal information as of July 2026. Panchayat boundaries, office contacts, and scheme rules can change — always confirm current details with the Udakishunganj Block/Anchal office or the official portal linked below before relying on them.

Udakishunganj community development block, in Madhepura district's Udakishunganj subdivision, is made up of gram panchayats covering a total of roughly 44 villages. Publicly available sources list the following gram panchayats within the block:

Treat the table above as a reference starting point, not a final legal record.

Notable villages in the block

Among the roughly 44 villages across these panchayats, some of the more commonly referenced include Uda, Rampur Khora, Nayanagar, Gamail, Pirnagar, and Bihariganj-adjacent settlements — each falling under one of the panchayats above depending on exact location.

Looking for your panchayat's Anganwadi, ration dealer, or scheme office? Start with your gram panchayat's mukhiya or panchayat sevak — they are the first point of contact for most village-level government services, from Anganwadi enrollment to MGNREGA job cards.

The bottom line

Udakishunganj block's panchayat structure has evolved over time like much of rural Bihar — for anything official (voter rolls, scheme eligibility, land records), always confirm the current panchayat and village boundary against madhepura.nic.in or your local panchayat office.
