Bihar Land Records, Jamabandi & Mutation (Dakhil-Kharij) Online: Guide for Udakishunganj

Updated August 2026 · Civic

This page compiles publicly available portal information from the Department of Revenue and Land Reforms, Government of Bihar, as of August 2026. Procedures and fees can change — confirm with the Circle (Anchal) office or the official portal.

Accurate land records matter for buying, selling, inheriting land, or applying for a bank loan against property. The Bihar government has moved most core services — khata, khesra, jamabandi, bhu-naksha, and dakhil-kharij (mutation) — online, letting residents of the Udakishunganj area check their land records without repeated visits to the Circle Office.

Official source: bhuabhilekh.bihar.gov.in (for viewing jamabandi and khatiyan records) and biharbhumi.bihar.gov.in (for mutation, LPC, and land-tax payment) — both run by the Department of Revenue and Land Reforms, Government of Bihar.

What you can check without logging in

  • Jamabandi register by khata and khesra number
  • Bhu Naksha (land map) for your plot
  • Khatiyan (old land record) copies

Getting a mutation (dakhil-kharij) done

After buying land or inheriting it, you must get the mutation done in your name. This requires creating a citizen login on biharbhumi.bihar.gov.in and uploading a scanned sale deed/will, the previous jamabandi number, and identity proof. The application goes to the Circle Office (the local Circle Officer for Udakishunganj), and a new jamabandi number is issued after verification.

Keep in mind: mutation typically takes several weeks and sometimes requires physical verification at the Circle Office. Before buying land, always check both jamabandi and khatiyan online to avoid disputes.

The bottom line

Land records are now largely accessible online, but mutation and dispute matters still require contacting the Circle Office. For more on the Circle and Block offices, see our BDO office guide.
