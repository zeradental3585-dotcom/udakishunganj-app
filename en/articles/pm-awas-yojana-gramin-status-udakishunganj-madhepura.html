PM Awas Yojana Gramin (PMAY-G): How to Check Status in Udakishunganj & Madhepura

Updated August 2026 · Scheme

This page compiles publicly available PMAY-G portal information from the Ministry of Rural Development as of August 2026. Beneficiary lists and scheme rules are updated by the central and state governments — check pmayg.nic.in for the latest details.

Under the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G), rural families who are homeless or living in kutcha (temporary) houses receive financial assistance to build a pucca (permanent) house. Under PMAY-G 2.0, the target is 2 crore additional rural homes nationwide by 2028–29. Eligible families in Udakishunganj block panchayats can check their status online.

Official source: pmayg.nic.in provides beneficiary lists, application status, and installment payment details — it is the official portal run by the central Ministry of Rural Development.

How to check your status

  • Visit pmayg.nic.in and select the "Stakeholders" tab from the top menu
  • Choose "IAY/PMAYG Beneficiary" from the dropdown and enter your registration number
  • Without a registration number, use "Advanced Search" — select state, district, block, and panchayat, and enter your name
  • The status shows your name, house allotment details, PMAY ID, and financial support received

Eligibility and how names get added to the beneficiary list

PMAY-G beneficiaries are selected based on Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) data and Gram Sabha approval. New applications cannot be submitted directly on the portal — eligible families should contact their Gram Panchayat and Awas Sahayak to learn the process for getting included in the list.

Keep in mind: house-construction funds are released in three installments based on construction progress. If an installment is delayed, first check your bank account and Aadhaar linking, then contact the panchayat's Awas Sahayak.

The bottom line

PMAY-G is the biggest support available for rural families building a pucca house. For other welfare schemes, see our Bihar Sarkari Yojana updates guide.
