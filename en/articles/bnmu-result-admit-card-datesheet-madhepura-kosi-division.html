BNMU Result, Admit Card & Datesheet 2026: Guide for Students in Madhepura, Saharsa & Supaul

Updated September 2026 · Education

This information is based on publicly available details on BNMU's website as of September 2026. Portal names and processes can change — always check bnmu.ac.in for the latest information.

Bhupendra Narayan Mandal University (BNMU), Madhepura is the main university for the Kosi division — covering Madhepura, Saharsa, and Supaul districts — serving hundreds of thousands of students. Since result, admit card, datesheet, and marksheet information is scattered across different portals, students often struggle to find what they need. This guide brings all the essential links and steps together in one place.

Official source: bnmu.ac.in (the university's main website and notices) and bnmuumis.in (the student result and UMIS portal) — both are official portals run by BNMU.

How to check your result

  • Visit bnmuumis.in or bnmu.ac.in and find the "Result" section
  • Select your course (UG/PG), semester, and session
  • Enter your college code, roll number, and registration number — these are available on your admit card or college ID
  • After submitting, your result/marksheet will display on screen — download and print the PDF for future reference

How to download your admit card

Students who have successfully submitted their exam form can download their admit card from bnmu.ac.in or bnmucertificate.in. The admit card lists your exam centre, date, and roll number — check all details carefully before the exam, and contact your college or the university exam department immediately if you spot an error.

Where to find the datesheet (exam schedule)

Semester and annual exam datesheets are published periodically by BNMU in the "Notice/Notification" section of bnmu.ac.in. Since datesheets change frequently, check the notice section regularly in the run-up to exams.

What to do if you don't have your college code or roll number

  • Contact your college's exam section
  • This information is available on your old marksheet, admission slip, or college ID card
  • You can also contact BNMU's Controller of Examination office

The bottom line

For thousands of students across the Kosi division (Madhepura, Saharsa, and Supaul), BNMU's result and admit card system is an everyday necessity. Bookmark this page for new datesheet and result updates. For school-level information, see our Bihar Board results guide.
