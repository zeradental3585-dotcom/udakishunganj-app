e-Shram Card Registration: Guide for Unorganised Workers — Udakishunganj & Madhepura

Updated September 2026 · Scheme

This information is based on publicly available details on the Ministry of Labour and Employment's e-Shram portal as of September 2026. Eligibility and linked schemes can change — check eshram.gov.in for the latest information.

For farm labourers, rickshaw/cart pullers, domestic workers, construction labourers, and other unorganised-sector workers in Udakishunganj and Madhepura district, the e-Shram card is an important identity document. It gives workers a Universal Account Number (UAN) and connects them to several welfare schemes.

Official source: eshram.gov.in — the official portal of the Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India. Registration is completely free.

Who can register (eligibility)

  • Age between 16 and 59 years
  • Working in the unorganised sector — such as farm labour, construction work, domestic work, rickshaw/auto driving, street vending, and similar occupations
  • Not a member of EPFO or ESIC
  • Aadhaar card linked to a mobile number

Benefits

  • A lifetime 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN)
  • Accident insurance cover of up to ₹2 lakh, linked to the Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana
  • Easier access to pension schemes such as Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan
  • Linkage to several central schemes including Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) and PM-SVANidhi
  • Priority for direct bank transfers of government relief during disasters

How to register online

  • Visit eshram.gov.in and click "Register on e-Shram"
  • Enter your Aadhaar-linked mobile number and verify with OTP
  • Fill in personal details, education, occupation, and bank account information
  • Download your e-Shram card with your UAN immediately after submitting the form
  • If you don't have a mobile phone or face difficulty online, free assistance is available at your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or Vasudha Kendra

Required documents

  • Aadhaar card (linked to a mobile number)
  • Bank account passbook (with IFSC code)
  • Mobile number (for OTP verification)
Keep in mind: e-Shram registration is completely free — don't pay any agent or cyber cafe extra for it. At a CSC, only the government-fixed service fee is payable.

The bottom line

For unorganised-sector workers in Udakishunganj and Madhepura, the e-Shram card is the first step toward accessing social security schemes. Keep your UAN safe after registering. For other welfare schemes, see our Bihar Sarkari Yojana updates guide.
