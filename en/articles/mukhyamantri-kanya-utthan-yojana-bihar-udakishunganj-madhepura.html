Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana: Financial Support for Girls — Udakishunganj & Madhepura Guide

Updated September 2026 · Scheme

This information is based on publicly available details on the Bihar Education Department's DBT portal as of September 2026. The exact instalment amounts and conditions have changed over time — always check edudbt.bih.nic.in for current information before applying.

The Bihar government's Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana provides financial assistance to every girl born in the state, across several stages from birth through graduation — regardless of the family's caste or income. This guide covers what families in Udakishunganj and Madhepura district need to know.

Official source: edudbt.bih.nic.in — the Bihar Education Department's DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) portal, where applications are made and funds are transferred directly to bank accounts. Helpline: 0612-2230059.

Which stages receive assistance

Support is provided at multiple stages — at birth, on completion of immunisation, on school admission, and for continuing education. The largest amounts come on passing 12th grade and completing graduation. The amounts below are for general reference — always check the official portal for exact, current figures:

StageSupport (approximate)
At birth₹2,000
Age 1 (immunisation complete)₹1,000
Age 2 (immunisation complete)₹2,000
Admission to Class 1₹2,000
Class 2 to 12 (annual, for uniforms)₹600 per year
Passing Class 12₹10,000
Completing graduation₹25,000

Eligibility

  • Must be a girl born in or resident of Bihar
  • No caste or family income limit — girls from all backgrounds are eligible
  • Documents required for the relevant stage (birth certificate, immunisation card, school/college enrolment proof) must be available

How to apply

  • For birth and early-stage support, contact your local Anganwadi centre or health department — beneficiary details are entered on the portal from there
  • School-level support is applied for through the relevant government/recognised school
  • For 12th-pass and graduation support, apply yourself online at edudbt.bih.nic.in
  • Enter Aadhaar and bank account details correctly — the amount is credited directly via DBT

Required documents

  • Birth certificate
  • Aadhaar card (of both the girl and guardian)
  • Bank passbook (in the girl's name or a joint account)
  • Enrolment or marksheet proof for the relevant class/stage
Keep in mind: the exact instalment amounts and conditions have changed over time — always check current rates on edudbt.bih.nic.in before applying, or call the helpline at 0612-2230059.

The bottom line

Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana is designed to encourage girls' education and reduce school dropout rates. For other education-related support, see our Bihar scholarship guide.
