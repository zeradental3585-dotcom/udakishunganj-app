Udakishunganj's MP & MLAs: Electoral Representatives Guide (Madhepura, Alamnagar & Bihariganj)

Updated August 2026 · Civic

This page compiles publicly available information as of August 2026 from the Election Commission of India and recognized election-data sources (such as ADR/MyNeta and IndiaVotes). This page provides factual electoral information only — it does not express any opinion for or against any party or candidate. Panchayat-level (Mukhiya/Ward Member) election results are scattered across sources and not included here — contact your local panchayat office or the State Election Commission, Bihar (sec.bihar.gov.in) for that information. Confirm with the Election Commission of India (eci.gov.in) before relying on this for any official purpose.

Udakishunganj block falls within Madhepura district, and electorally sits under the Madhepura Lok Sabha constituency and two assembly constituencies — Alamnagar (covering panchayats including Gopalpur, Rahta Phanhan, Budhma, Nayanagar, Khara, Sahjadpur, Jotaili, Lashkari, and Manjora) and Bihariganj (covering panchayats including Biriranpal, Pipra Karoti, Madhuban, Barahi Anadpura, Rampur Khora, Lakshmipur, and Kishunganj).

Official source: the Election Commission of India (eci.gov.in) and the Chief Electoral Officer Bihar (ceobihar.nic.in) publish official election results. Recognized independent, verifiable election data is also available via ADR/MyNeta (myneta.info) and IndiaVotes.

MLA — Alamnagar Assembly Constituency

2025 election: Narendra Narayan Yadav (JD-U) won, with a margin of 55,465 votes. Runner-up was Nabin Kumar (VIP).

2020 election (previous): Narendra Narayan Yadav (JD-U) — 102,517 votes (48.17%) — defeated Nabin Kumar (RJD) — 73,837 votes (34.69%) — by a margin of 28,680 votes.

MLA — Bihariganj Assembly Constituency

2025 election: Niranjan Kumar Mehta (JD-U) — 116,622 votes — won. Runner-up was Renu Kushwaha (RJD), losing by a margin of about 31,622 votes.

2020 election (previous): Niranjan Kumar Mehta (JD-U) defeated Congress's Subhashini Bundela (alias Subhashini Sharad Yadav) by a margin of 18,711 votes.

Keep in mind: Madhepura district has two more assembly constituencies — Madhepura (won by RJD's Chandra Shekhar in 2025) and Singheshwar-SC (won by JD-U's Ramesh Rishi in 2025) — which don't directly cover Udakishunganj block, but are frequently asked about given their connection to the district headquarters and surrounding administration.

Where to find panchayat-level representatives

Election results for panchayat-level representatives like Mukhiya, Sarpanch, and Ward Members aren't easily available in a centralized state-level database. For information on your panchayat's current Mukhiya and members, contact your local panchayat office, the Block office, or the State Election Commission Bihar website (sec.bihar.gov.in).
