Aadhaar Card Update & Enrolment Centres: Where to Go in Udakishunganj & Madhepura

Updated August 2026 · Directory

This page compiles publicly available UIDAI information as of August 2026. Aadhaar centre addresses and services can change — confirm on the myAadhaar portal or via the 1947 helpline before visiting.

Aadhaar has become a near-mandatory document for ration cards, bank accounts, Voter ID, school admissions, and almost every government scheme. For residents of Udakishunganj and Madhepura district, several corrections — like linking a mobile number — can be started online, while biometric updates require an in-person visit to a centre.

Official source: UIDAI's myAadhaar portal (myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) handles online address/name corrections; biometric updates (fingerprint, iris, photo) require visiting a nearby Aadhaar Seva Kendra or enrolment centre.

What can be done online

  • Address, name spelling, date of birth, email — via OTP verification on the myAadhaar portal (some corrections need supporting documents)
  • Linking or changing your mobile number — this must be done in person at a centre, it is not OTP-based
  • Downloading e-Aadhaar and ordering an Aadhaar PVC card

What requires a visit to a centre

Biometric updates (fingerprint/iris scan, photo change), mobile number registration, and the mandatory biometric updates required at ages 5 and 15 all require visiting a nearby Aadhaar Seva Kendra, a bank-branch Aadhaar enrolment centre, or a Vasudha Kendra. Several bank branches in Madhepura district — including Central Bank of India, Uttar Bihar Gramin Bank, and UCO Bank — offer Aadhaar services.

Keep in mind: children's Aadhaar (Bal Aadhaar) must be biometrically updated at ages 5 and 15, or the card can become inactive. Check this before school admission season.

The bottom line

Minor corrections can be done online via the myAadhaar portal, but biometric updates and mobile linking require an in-person visit. A correct Aadhaar record matters for ration cards and other documents too — see our ration card guide.
