About Udakishunganj.com

Udakishunganj.com is a hyperlocal community information site covering Udakishunganj block and subdivision in Madhepura district, Bihar — practical guides on government offices, welfare schemes, ration cards, Anganwadi services, and local directory information.

How we work

Every guide on this site links directly to the relevant official government portal or district administration page (madhepura.nic.in, EPDS Bihar, Poshan Tracker, and similar) rather than presenting itself as an official source. Our role is to make practical local information easier to find, not to replace official channels.

Independence

Udakishunganj.com is not affiliated with the Madhepura district administration, the Bihar state government, or any government department. Always verify scheme eligibility, office contacts, and administrative boundaries on the official portals linked in each guide.
